About Me

A few of the main things I enjoy doing in my free time:

SkiingHaving lived in Colorado for my entire life, skiing was the first sport or outdoor activity I found enjoyable. I've been skiing since 2015 at the age of 6 and its been a major part of my life ever since. Most years you can find me on the mountain at Loveland Ski resort with my favorite run being Golden Bear Peak.
TheaterTheater has been a more recent interest of mine, having joined the columbine high school "rebels with applause" theater program in my sophomore year. I mainly work on set design and construction, having been the construction lead for 4 productions. The community and sense of belonging in the theater community made it a major part of my life.
MusicWhile not a musician myself, the theater community has introduced me to a large variety of music and bands. Going to the occasional concert has become one of my favorite things to do.
